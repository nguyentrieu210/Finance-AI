# Release gates
G00 bootstrap; G01 contract freeze: finance owner confirms formulas and policy matrix, security owner confirms trust boundaries, engineering owner reviews OpenAPI/schema/ports. If missing inputs, freeze only technical prototype assumptions marked draft; never pilot business approval. G02 real DB security/audit/jobs. G03 integrated three-kind flow and concurrency. G04 approved pilot using manual handoff and independently verified references; baseline/target NFRs in spec; evidence reports, no critical open defects, restore drill. G05 live DO/ERP only with credentials, sandbox and verified contracts, operational rollback and support ownership. No transfer endpoint. SaaS billing, multi-region, advanced anomaly ML, arbitrary low-code execution and full accounting ledger excluded.

Owner decisions required: policy checklist and signed clauses, forms, source ownership/completeness, rounding/FX/tax/retention, approval order DO vs accounting, identity and delegation, storage retention/legal holds, AI egress, SLO/scale, DO/ERP API. No external dependency assumed solved. Test evidence and target measurements explicitly distinct.
